MY JOLLOF RICE RECIPE

By Davvd .O

The items, ingredients and step by step intructions below are on how to make Nigerian jollof , a populer dish in West Africa.

It is made by a rich blend of tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, onions, spices and stock.

The secret to achieving delicious Nigerian Jollof Rice is in the preperation of the pepper base, proper seasoning and controlled cooking.

COOKING ITEMS

  • A Cooking pot with a lid
  • A Blender
  • A Kitchen knife
  • A Mesuring cup
  • A wooden spoon (for stirring the ingredients)
  • Sieve (washing and draining rice)
  • A frying spoon (for handling the stew)
  • A Chopping board
  • Aluminium foil (covering the pot during cooking)
  • Kitchen grater

INGREDIENTS

  • Rice
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Red bell peppers(Tatashe)
  • Scotch bonnet peppers (Rodo)
  • Tomatoe paste
  • Onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken or beef stock
  • Butter (optional)
  • Curry powder
  • Thyme
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt to taste
  • Bay leaves (optional)
  • Ginger

STEPES ON HOW TO GO ABOUT IT

  1. Step 1- The Pepper Mixture: Wash the tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, and onions. Cut them into smaller pieces and blend until smooth.
  2. Step 2- The Rice: Wash the rice throughly with clean water to remove excess starch. Parboil if necessary, then drain and set aside.
  3. Step 3- Cook The Tomatoe Sauce: Heat the vegetable oil in a cooking pot. Add chopped onions and tomatoe paste. Fry for a few minutes, stirring regularly. Add the blended pepper mixture and cook until the sauce thickens and excess water reduces.
  4. Step 4- Add Seasoning: Add curry powder, thyme, seasoning cubes, salt. and bay leaves. Stir properly to combine the ingredients. Pour in the Chicken or Beef or water.
  5. Step 5- Add The Rice: Add the washed rice to the tomatoe sauce and stir until the rice is evenly coated. Ensure that the liquid is sufficient to cook the rice without making it soggy.
  6. Step 6- Cook The Rice: Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and cook on low to medium heat. Stir occasionally to prevent burning. Add a little water if the rice becomes too dry before it is cooked.
  7. Step 7- Serve: When the rice is soft and fully cooked, remove it from the heat. Allow to rest briefly before serving. Enjoy your Nigerian jollof rice with fried plantain, grilled Chicken, or salad.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTES

  • knife Safety: Always handle knives carefully when cutting vegetables.
  • Hot oil warning: Never pure water directly into hot oil,as this can cause a dengerous splashing and burns.
  • Food hygine: Wash your hands and Kitchen equipments before handling food.
  • Food Safety: The rice and any chicken stock used are properly cooked, and safely stored.

COOKING TERMINOLOGIES

Parboiling
Partially cooking rice in boiling water before completing the cooking process.
Sauteing
Cooking ingredients quickly in a small amount of hot oil while stirring.
Simmering
Cooking food gently in liquid at a temperature just below boiling point.
Seasoning
Adding salt, spices, herbs, or other ingredients to improve the flavour of food.

Conclusion

Nigerian Jollof Rice is a delicious and sartisfying meal that can be prepared with simple ingredients. By following these steps carefully, you can prepare a tasty meal for your family and friends.
